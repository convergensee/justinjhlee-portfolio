I'm currently a Senior Product Marketing Manager at SAP, where I lead go-to-market strategy and customer adoption for AI capabilities on the SAP Business Technology Platform. My work sits at the intersection of enterprise technology, analyst relations, and commercial storytelling.

Over the past decade I've worked across B2B SaaS, digital marketing, and data analytics — always with an eye on how to make complex technology feel inevitable to the right audience.
